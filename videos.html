I learn partly by watching — a diagram being drawn while someone talks lands better for me than the same thing in prose. This is the record of what I have watched, when, and what it was actually good for. The channels are the vetted ones from RESOURCES.md, not whatever the algorithm offered.

The rule I hold to: video is for intuition, learncpp is for style. Where a video's syntax disagrees with learncpp, learncpp wins. This matters most with The Cherno, who uses raw new and delete throughout — correct inside Project 1, where writing a container means I am the one doing manual memory, and not to be carried into Projects 2 and 3.
